# v36work Automated Preliminary Review

The automated assessment is decision support only. It does not approve or deny a request and does not replace the two independent reviewer assessments.

## Eligibility first filter
Eligibility is shown separately from the score. The system checks the reported length of CCI connection, membership/MAP evidence, pastoral confirmation of current participation, and Unit Head verification for Celeforce workers. Cases below the standard three-month duration or with verification problems are flagged for attention/escalation.

## Seven Appendix 1 scoring criteria (maximum 21)
1. Urgency: pre-filled from existing urgency triage and deadline/risk indicators.
2. Severity of Impact: uses structured risk indicators, essential-need category, and whether dependents are affected.
3. Financial Gap: calculates total need minus applicant contribution and confirmed outside support, then compares the CCI amount requested with the remaining gap.
4. Applicant Effort: uses structured actions selected by the applicant, narrative explanation, and personal contribution.
5. History of Assistance: queries prior approved cases in the app database, including approvals in the current calendar year and time since the most recent approval.
6. Policy Alignment: checks covered categories, selected category limits where the policy provides numerical limits, the calculated gap, annual assistance history, and exception conditions.
7. Suitability of Documentation: checks whether required application documents and verification responses are present. Human reviewers still judge authenticity, quality, and credibility.

## Reviewer control
Every score is pre-filled but editable. A reviewer who selects Partially/No for agreement, or changes any pre-filled score, must provide a reason. The system stores a snapshot of its preliminary assessment alongside the review for auditability.

## Important policy handling
The Appendix 1 form has seven scored criteria, so the automated scored total is /21. Eligibility remains a separate first filter. The appendix's published interpretation guide does not explicitly cover 19-21, so v36work labels 19-21 as a very strong preliminary case while clearly identifying it as above the stated 15-18 strong range. Final decisions remain human decisions.
